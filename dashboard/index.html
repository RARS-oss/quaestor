03 What makes this different

Not a backtest. A chain of evidence.

Five properties compose into one guarantee: you can reconstruct and check exactly what the agent did, without trusting the agent, the operator, or us.

Sealed live trading

Each order is placed inside a hermetic no-root cell. TLS terminates in the seal; egress is mediated to paper-api.alpaca.markets:443.

seal HELD

Signed receipts

The exact request/response with the exchange is captured and Ed25519-signed into a hash-chained ledger — a tamper-evident record of every trade.

Ed25519 · SHA-256

ZK risk proofs

A zero-knowledge proof, bound to the client_order_id, attests worst-case loss stayed under 216 USD — without revealing the position.

Bulletproofs

External anchor

The ledger head is anchored to an independent timestamp, so history can't be quietly rewritten after the fact — the past is pinned.

anchored head

WASM verify

The verifier compiles from the same Rust to WebAssembly, so anyone re-checks a receipt in the browser — no toolchain, no install, no trust.

verify anywhere
