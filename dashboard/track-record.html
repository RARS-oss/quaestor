Disclosure

Paper trading. quaestor runs against the Alpaca paper environment (paper-api.alpaca.markets) — a fail-closed gate refuses to start against a live host. Fills are simulated at the NBBO touch with zero fees, so equity here reflects paper marks, not realized cash.

What the proof does and doesn't say. A verifying receipt proves the agent executed exactly the inputs and orders recorded, inside a hermetic cell, signed by the key shown above, in an unbroken chain anchored to an external witness. It does not predict future returns. Past agent performance is evidence of process integrity, not a guarantee of profit.

Verification is local. The check runs entirely in your browser via the embedded WebAssembly build of bulla verify. Nothing is sent anywhere; you can save this page and verify offline.
