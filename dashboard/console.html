Verify it yourself

None of this asks for trust. Re-check every signature and ledger chain offline, with no Alpaca credentials:

# re-check every receipt signature + ledger chain, offline
make verify
python -m quaestor verify
# or open the in-browser verifier and tamper with a receipt
open dashboard/verifier.html

Not investment advice. Options trading involves significant risk. Paper-trading only; simulated results. A verifying receipt proves the agent executed exactly the inputs and orders recorded, inside a hermetic cell, signed by the key shown above — it does not predict future returns.
